# Genesis.ai Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Genesis.ai ("Genesis.ai", "we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our website, applications, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to personal information processed through Genesis.ai Services, including account registration, AI-powered project generation, GitHub integrations, preview/deployment features, and support communications.

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

### A. Account and Profile Information

- Name and email address.
- Password hash (we do not store plain-text passwords).
- Profile image URL or uploaded avatar image.
- Account metadata (for example, created/updated timestamps and verification status).

### B. Authentication and Security Data

- Login and session data such as authentication tokens.
- OAuth-related identifiers from third-party sign-in providers (for example, GitHub ID).
- OAuth access tokens you connect for integrations (for example, GitHub token).
- Email verification, password reset, and similar account-security records.

### C. Project and Workspace Data

- Project names, prompts, selected AI model, stack settings, and generation/edit status.
- Generated and user-edited source files stored as part of your projects.
- Repository/deployment metadata such as GitHub repository URL, deployment URL, deployment status, and logs.

### D. Uploaded Files

- Profile images uploaded to the Services.

### E. Technical and Usage Data

- IP address and request metadata processed for security, fraud prevention, and rate limiting.
- Basic device/browser and server log information generated in connection with service operation.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Provide, maintain, and improve the Services.
- Create and manage user accounts.
- Authenticate users and protect account security.
- Generate, edit, preview, and deploy software projects.
- Send transactional emails (for example, verification, welcome, and password reset messages).
- Diagnose issues, prevent abuse, enforce policies, and comply with legal obligations.

## 4. AI Model Processing

When you submit prompts or project-edit instructions, we may send that content to configured third-party AI model providers to generate project output. Your prompts and related context are processed for the purpose of producing requested results.

Available providers may include, based on your configured environment: Google, OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral, and xAI.

## 5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

- Service providers who support infrastructure, email delivery, AI processing, and deployment workflows.
- Third-party integrations you choose to connect (for example, GitHub).
- Hosting/deployment platforms used by you through our deployment features (for example, Vercel or Render).
- Legal authorities when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

## 6. Legal Bases (Where Applicable)

If you are in a jurisdiction requiring a legal basis for processing (such as the EEA/UK), we rely on one or more of the following:

- Performance of a contract (to provide Services you request).
- Legitimate interests (security, product improvement, abuse prevention).
- Consent (where required).
- Compliance with legal obligations.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide Services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Retention may vary by data type. For example:

- Account and project data: retained while account is active, and for a limited period thereafter.
- Logs and diagnostics: retained for security and operational purposes for a limited duration.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data, subject to legal and operational requirements.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. International Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including where our providers operate. Where required, we implement appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

## 10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

- Access your personal information.
- Correct inaccurate information.
- Delete your information.
- Restrict or object to certain processing.
- Request data portability.
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise rights, contact us using the details below.

## 11. Children

The Services are not directed to children under 13 (or older minimum age required by local law). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version with a revised "Last updated" date.
